Vasudha Nukala

I am an Assistant Professor of Finance at the Indian School of Business. I received my PhD from Olin Business School, Washington University in St. Louis.

My research studies how information and financial institutions shape credit allocation, household financial decisions, and firms' access to capital.

Working Papers

Transaction Information and Credit Allocation in Buy Now, Pay Later

FinTech · Consumer credit
Abstract

We study how transaction-level information affects screening and the allocation of consumer credit in buy now, pay later lending. Using lender application and loan data and randomized approvals, the project examines the information content of transaction signals and their implications for access to credit and borrower outcomes.

Uncertain Times: Entrants, Incumbents, and Miners in the Bitcoin Network

Cryptocurrency · Investor behavior
Abstract

This project examines how economic uncertainty affects entry and trading behavior in the Bitcoin network, distinguishing new users, existing holders, and miners.
