Vasudha Nukala

Assistant Professor of Finance · Indian School of Business

My research is broadly within economics and finance, focusing on financial intermediation, household finance and fintech. My work focusses on how credit allocation happens in consumer and corporate credit markets, with a focus on how technology and regulation shape the information dynamics.

I earned my Ph.D. in Finance from Olin Business School, Washington University in St. Louis. Before that, I graduated with a M.S in Finance from Simon Business School, University of Rochester. I did my undergraduate education in Engineering and Economics from Birla Institute of Technology and Science, Pilani, India. My work experience includes working at Cornerstone Research in New York, Ernst & Young in Detroit and Deloitte in New York.

Research

Working Papers

Transaction Information and Credit Allocation in Buy Now, Pay Later

FinTech · Consumer credit · Financial Intermediation · Information Economics
Abstract

We study how transaction-level information affects screening and the allocation of consumer credit in buy now, pay later lending. Using lender application and loan data and randomized approvals, the project examines the information content of transaction signals and their implications for access to credit and borrower outcomes.

Uncertain Times: Entrants, Incumbents, and Miners in the Bitcoin Network

Cryptocurrency · Investor behavior
Abstract

This project examines how economic uncertainty affects entry and trading behavior in the Bitcoin network, distinguishing new users, existing holders, and miners.
